## Slide 4: Persona 1 – Mobilizing Skills & Career Opportunities

### *"From Resume to Real-World Ecological Restoration"*
* **Capacity & Skill Parsing:** Ingests volunteer resumes, LinkedIn profiles, or natural conversation to detect capabilities (e.g. GIS mapping, hydrology, curriculum design).
* **Automated Civic Matchmaking:** Connects specialists to permitted, high-impact campaigns (e.g., matching a civil engineer to review bioswale trench blueprints).
* **Workshop Co-Pilot:** Assists educators in co-authoring community science workshops and field protocols.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> "Instead of filling out generic volunteer forms that sit in an inbox, the agent immediately matches a user's specific skillsets to verified local campaign needs, syncing calendar dates and generating orientation briefs."

---

## Slide 5: Persona 2 – Democratizing Environmental Knowledge

### *"Adaptive Socratic Explainer & Multimodal Visualizer"*
* **Multi-Tiered Explanations:** Breaks down complex Santa Clara Valley Water District metrics (e.g., aquifer drawdown, nitrate PPM) from ELI5 to technical depth.
* **Before/After AI Generation:** Uses Imagen to visually project restorative outcomes (e.g., asphalt parking lot transformed into permeable bioswale canopy).
* **Hydrological Impact Sandbox:** Runs live Python calculations to model runoff reduction and acre-feet recharge.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> "For learners and educators, the agent functions as an open science educator. It demystifies local data and generates compelling visual media that local advocates can use in classrooms and town halls."

---

## Slide 6: Persona 3 – Transparent & Accountable Capital

### *"Connecting Dollars Directly to Ecological Yield"*
* **Quantifiable ROI Calculator:** Translates donations into physical metrics ($100 = 250 sq ft bioswale = 12,000 gallons stormwater filtered/year).
* **Milestone Due Diligence:** Itemizes funding tiers, municipal permits, and procurement stages.
* **Frictionless Backing:** One-click pledge cards with automated tax receipts and milestone alerts.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> "For donors, transparency is paramount. The agent eliminates guesswork by showing exactly how many gallons of water are recharged or square feet of soil are restored for every dollar contributed."
