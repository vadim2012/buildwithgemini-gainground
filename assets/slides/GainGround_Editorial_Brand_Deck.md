## Slide 3: The 3 User Flows on the Editorial Canvas

![GainGround 3 User Flows](/config/.gemini/antigravity/brain/b5e4ffc3-1b8c-4cdf-baa3-3ee8942437cd/slide_editorial_3flows_1791496765613.png)

### 1. Talent & Volunteers (The Mobilizer)
* **Intelligent Skill Parsing:** Detects hydrology, GIS mapping, ecological restoration, or teaching abilities.
* **Champion Matchmaking:** Direct alignment to campaign leads like Elena Chen with a 98% skill fit score.
* **Field Briefings:** Automated calendar sync and orientation guides for upcoming volunteer workdays.

### 2. Learners & Educators (The Knowledge Engine)
* **Geological Strata Breakdown:** Translates complex municipal groundwater reports into multi-layer Socratic insights.
* **Drawdown & Nitrate Alerts:** Highlights contamination hotspots and explains recharge mechanics.
* **Educational Exports:** Scaffolds classroom quizzes and one-pagers for teachers and local content creators.

### 3. Donors & Financiers (Accountable Capital)
* **Kickstarter-Style Transparency:** Real-time progress bars with itemized milestone audits.
* **Quantifiable ROI Metrics:** Converts pledges directly into cubic yards of bioswale restored and gallons of water recharged.
* **Frictionless Backing:** Instant tax receipts and ongoing milestone alerts delivered directly to funders.
